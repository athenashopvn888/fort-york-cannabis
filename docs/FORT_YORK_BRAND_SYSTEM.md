# Fort York Cannabis Brand System

## 1. Brand Positioning

FORT YORK CANNABIS should feel like a polished downtown Toronto cannabis storefront built for Fort York, CityPlace, the waterfront, King West, and nearby condo traffic. The brand should earn trust quickly, support local buyer intent, and feel retail-ready without using fake claims or cannabis cliches.

## 2. Visual Mood

Modern urban retail, waterfront at night, Fort York masonry, condo-district precision, and clean adult-use cannabis presentation. The look should be confident and local, not cartoonish, not cheap, and not copied from another store.

## 4. Typography Direction

Use strong geometric sans-serif display styling for headlines, supported by clean system sans-serif body text. Preferred direction: uppercase logo and wayfinding text, tight but readable headings, and restrained body copy. Avoid decorative fonts.

## 5. Logo Rules

The FYC mark should be used as a monogram badge for favicon, age gate, mobile header, and small signage. The full wordmark should read FORT YORK CANNABIS with Fort York and Cannabis stacked or separated by a copper rule. Do not add leaves, smoke mascots, fake awards, or unverified license language.

## 6. Icon/Favicon Rules

Favicons should use the FYC monogram, dark base, copper border, and teal accent. Keep tiny versions high contrast and legible. Do not include address, phone, hours, or detailed artwork in favicon assets.

## 7. Website Art Direction

Website visuals should use structured architectural artwork: skyline blocks, fort-wall masonry, waterfront bands, copper transit/grid lines, and clean retail panels. The current assets are first-pass production-intent coded raster artwork, not final sign-shop production art.

## 8. Banner Art Direction

Banners should avoid random circles/lines. Use consistent Fort York visual ingredients: masonry wall rhythm, downtown skyline, waterfront reflection bands, and copper/teal framing. Each banner can change accent emphasis by section or category.

## 9. Category Card Art Direction

Category cards should feel like curated retail tiles, not product inventory claims. They may show broad category names only until inventory is confirmed. No product prices, brands, potency, menu promises, or stock claims until owner-approved data exists.

## 10. Storefront Signage Direction

Main sign should be high-contrast, legible from street distance, and built around the full wordmark. Recommended: dark fascia, copper border or trim, white FORT YORK text, teal CANNABIS text, and optional domain/address secondary line.

## 11. Window/Door Signage Direction

Door signage can include Adults 19+ and ID-required messaging. Window vinyl can use the Fort York wall/harbour pattern and the FYC mark. Hours decal must remain pending until owner confirms final hours.

## 12. Local SEO/GEO Visual Content Rules

Visuals must reinforce Fort York, CityPlace, waterfront, downtown Toronto, King West, and nearby condo traffic. Do not imply unverified delivery, pickup, open-now status, late-night hours, ratings, reviews, or license status.

## 13. What Not To Do

- Do not copy another store visually.
- Do not rely on random circles, blobs, or decorative lines as the identity.
- Do not use fake storefront photos, fake staff, fake reviews, fake ratings, fake hours, or fake license claims.
- Do not overuse cannabis leaves or smoke cliches.
- Do not use cheap neon nightclub styling.
- Do not publish confirmed hours, phone, delivery, pickup, menu, or license wording until owner approval.
